# AOCA Engineering Consultants — marketing site

Client site for AOCA (Aidan O'Connell & Associates), a multidisciplinary
engineering consultancy (est. 1996, Portlaoise/Dublin/Manchester) serving
Ireland, the UK and Europe. Audience: developers, local authorities,
insurers, contractors and homeowners commissioning engineering work.

- register: brand (marketing site — design communicates credibility)
- Voice: assured, plain-spoken, engineer-first. "Straight talking" is a
  company value; copy avoids agency fluff.
- Goal per page: convince a commissioning client this firm is competent and
  established, then route them to the contact form / phone.
- Content model: all copy lives in `lib/site.ts`; client edits via /admin
  land in `content/*.json` overrides. Nine expertise areas (Aug 2026 deck),
  ~35 projects across seven sectors, real testimonials and bios.
- Staging: https://aoca-draft.vercel.app (Vercel). Production domain
  aoca.ie post-approval; go-live steps in GO-LIVE.md, outstanding content
  in PLACEHOLDERS.md.
